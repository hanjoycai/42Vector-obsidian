## 了解 Prompts

> [!note] 📌
> 前言
> 
> Prompt是什么，他是日常表达能力的一个映射。
> 
> 日常中跟人沟通能力很强的人，其实不知道这些技巧，一样能跟大模型协同的很好。
> 
> 表达清楚自己的需求，与人协同顺利，才是最重要的。
> 
> > OpenAl提到6条大的原则，分别是：
> 
> `1.`Write clear instructions （写出清晰的指令）
> 
> `2.`Provide reference text(提供参考文本）
> 
> `3.`Split complex tasks into simpler subtasks (将复杂的任务拆分为更简单的子任务）
> 
> `4.`Give the model time to “think“ (给模型时间《思考》）
> 
> `5.`Use external tools （使用外部工具）
> 
> `6.`Test changes systematically（系统地测试变更）

> [!note] ⌛
> **Auto Archive**
> **Archive** is a place to view and access closed tabs. Today’s tabs will automatically archive after a set amount of time (default is 12 hours) — you can restore anything you miss from the Archive.
> 
> > **To access the Archive**
> 
> - Click History → “View Archive”
> - `⌘` + `T` → “View Archive”
> 
> > **To adjust the timing of auto-Archive **
> 
> - Go to Arc → Preference → Archive

> **Folders**

> > **To create a new Folder:**

> - **Click the **`**+**`** icon **at the bottom of the sidebar

> [!note] 📡
> **Spaces**
> **Almost** **like unique sidebars — **they have their own Pinned and Today’s Tabs sections**. **Spaces allow you to separate different browsing contexts and are the best way to organize big areas of your life.
> 
> > **To create a new Space:**
> 
> - **Click the **`**+**`** icon **at the bottom of the sidebar
> - `⌘` + `T` and type "New Space..."
> > [!note]+ **To switch Spaces**
> > > **To switch Spaces:**
> > 
> > - **Click on the Space’s icon at the bottom of the sidebar**
> > - If you have a trackpad, **two-finger swipe left or right.**
> > - `CTRL` + *[Space Number (1 = far left)]*
> > - `⌘` + `T` and type "Focus on *[Space Name]*"
> > 

<!-- Column 1 -->
> [!note] ⭐
> **Favorites**
> **Tabs you want to access all the time — **things like calendar or email. They are the only tabs that are not confined to a specific Space! 
> 
> > **To Favorite a tab:**
> 
> - **Drag a tab** to the area below the address bar
>     - `⌘` + `T` and type "Favorite this Tab"

> [!note] 👥
> **Profiles**
> 
> **Profiles** allow you to scope Logins, Cookies, Browsing History, and other settings to one or multiple Spaces. 
> 
> A common use for Profiles is separating Work data from Personal data.
> 
> > [!note]+ **What’s included in a Profile:**
> > - Authentication (logins, passwords, autofill)
> > - Browser History
> > - Cache / Cookies
> > - Default Browser Selection
> > - Favorites
> > - Extensions
> > - Settings in arc://settings (such as Language or Download Preferences)
> 
> **To add a Profile to a Space:**
> 
> For now, Profiles do not sync across devices (but we hope to build this soon!). Your Spaces and Tabs will still sync across devices, even if they belong to a Profile.
> 
> [Click here for a full video overview of how to use Profiles and Spaces.](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kVZZv1ygNP8)

## 方法框架

> [!note] 🪓
> **Split View**
> **View up to 4 vertical tabs at once** — multitasking is easier than ever!
> 
> > **To enter Split View:**
> 
> - Drag a tab from the sidebar into the middle of the screen
> - `⌘ T` → "Add Split View"
> - `Control` + `Shift` + `+`
> 
> > **To exit Split View:**
> 
> - `⌘ T` → "Close this Split View"
> - `Control` + `Shift` + `—`

> [!note] 🎶
> **Notes**
> Arc has built in **Notes **so you can open a quick scratchpad whenever. 
> 
> You can also share a viewable version of your Note, even with people who don't have Arc. 
> 
> 
> > [!note]+ **To create a note:**
> > - **Click the **`**+**`** icon **at the bottom of the sidebar
> > - `Control` + `N`
> > - `⌘` + `T` → "New Note"
> 
> > [!note]+ **To share a note:**
> > - Click "Share Link to Note" in the address bar, which will generate and copy a URL that you can share.

> [!note] 🎨
> **Easel / Capture**
> **Easel** is a super simple canvas tool for creating on and with the internet. You can write text, draw, add shapes, etc. 
> 
> 
> Where **Easel** really shines is when paired with **Capture **— a way to snippet parts of the internet to save for later.
> 
> 
> > [!note]+ **Create an Easel:**
> > - **Click the **`**+**`** icon **at the bottom of the sidebar
> > - `Control` + `Shift` + `E`
> > - `⌘ T` → "New Easel"
> 
> > [!note]+ **To Capture:**
> > - **Click the camera icon** in the address bar and click + drag (like a screenshot)
> > - Shift ⌘ + Drag (like a screenshot)

> [!note] 🎥
> **Mini Player**
> Continue watching or listening to something even if you click away from the tab.
> 
> > **To use Mini Player:**
> 
> - Click away from a tab playing a video or audio, you’ll notice a small controller appear in the bottom of the sidebar.
>     - If it’s a video, you’ll also notice a picture-in-picture window appear.
> 
> > **To turn off Mini Player:**
> 
> - Click the lock icon on any site and uncheck “Mini Player”.

> [!note] 📆
> **Previews**
> With **Previews** you can view upcoming meetings, and even join Zoom calls, right from your the sidebar! 
> 
> > **To set up a Preview**
> 
> - Hover over the tab and grant access if necessary.
>     - You can have **Previews **for: 
>         - *Google Calendar*
>         - *Gmail*
>         - *Outlook *
>         - *GitHub*
>         - *Notion*
>         - *Figma*
>         - *Linear*
> 
> > **To turn off a Preview**
> 
> - Go to **Arc → Preferences**
> - `⌘ T` → ”Preferences”
