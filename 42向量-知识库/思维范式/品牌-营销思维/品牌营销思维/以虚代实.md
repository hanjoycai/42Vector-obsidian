**50. REPLACE A REAL EXPERIENCE WITH A VIRTUAL ONE****50.以虚代实**

We now live in a time when it’s possible to create any experience through speakers and screens.我们现在生活在一个可以通过扬声器和屏幕创造任何体验的时代。

Think of places you can now travel to with the help of digital technology that you couldn’t go before.想一想，在数字技术的帮助下，你现在可以去哪些以前去不了的地方旅行。

Think of the things you can do now that could only be done in the past by the fortunate, the wealthy or the physically fit.想想你现在能做的事情，而这些事情在过去只有幸运儿、富人或身体健康的人才能做到。

You have the power to transport your audience into the past, into the future, into outer space, across the oceans, to the bottom of the sea, into make-believe land, into each other’s loving arms or even inside the cluttered, conflicted head of the President of the USA.您有能力将观众带入过去、未来、外太空、大洋彼岸、海底、虚幻之地、彼此相爱的怀抱，甚至是美国总统杂乱无章、矛盾重重的内心世界。

All you have to do is figure out your destination. And make the trip emotional.你所要做的就是确定目的地。让旅行充满情感。
