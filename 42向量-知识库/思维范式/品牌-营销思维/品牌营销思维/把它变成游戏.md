**44. TURN IT INTO A GAME****44.把它变成游戏**

Turn your project into something fun, engaging and rewarding: a game.将您的项目变成有趣、吸引人和有价值的东西：游戏。

Anything can be ‘gamified’.任何事情都可以 "游戏化"。

‘Gamification’, in essence, is about giving people a target to work towards and rewarding their efforts as they progress.从本质上讲，"游戏化 "就是给人们提供一个努力的目标，并在他们取得进步时给予奖励。

Games tap into many of our natural instincts–our optimism, our desire to do something extraordinary, our willingness to collaborate with others, our resilience when we fail and the satisfaction we get from going past a finish line.游戏激发了我们的许多天性--我们的乐观主义、我们对非凡事物的渴望、我们与他人合作的意愿、我们在失败时的韧性以及我们在冲过终点线时获得的满足感。

Games can also be a way to change behavior, to discourage bad habits or encourage positive ones.游戏也可以是改变行为、阻止坏习惯或鼓励积极习惯的一种方式。

And games don’t necessarily have to be competitive.游戏不一定非得是竞技性的。

In fact, studies show that collaborative games have more appeal than competitive ones.事实上，研究表明，合作性游戏比竞争性游戏更具吸引力。

Nor do games have to be based on fantasy.游戏也不一定要基于幻想。

Reality-based, non-fiction games can also be attractive.基于现实的非虚构游戏也很有吸引力。
