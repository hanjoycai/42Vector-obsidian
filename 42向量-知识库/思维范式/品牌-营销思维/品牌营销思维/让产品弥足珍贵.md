**08. MAKE THE PRODUCT PRECIOUS****08.让产品弥足珍贵**

Pretend that whatever you are selling is extremely rare and hard to get hold of.假装你要卖的东西非常罕见，很难买到。

You might imagine that it’s the last such item of its kind in the world and they’ll never make another like it.你可能会想，这是世界上最后一件同类产品了，他们再也不会生产类似的产品了。

Now construct scenarios involving crazed fans willing to do anything to possess this object.现在，我们来构建一个场景，让疯狂的粉丝们不惜一切代价拥有这件物品。

Make them wait for it. Make them fight for it. Make them cheat, lie, suffer, sacrifice, steal and kill for it.让他们等待。让他们为之奋斗。让他们为之欺骗、撒谎、受苦、牺牲、偷窃和杀戮。

Then justify this madness by pointing out that your product is worth it all.然后为这种疯狂行为辩解，指出你的产品物有所值。

Your justification could be the superior ingredients, taste and quality of your offering.你的理由可以是产品的上乘配料、口味和质量。

Or the superior status it confers on its users.或者是它赋予使用者的优越地位。

Somehow in the world of advertising, you get away with it.在广告界，不知何故，你却能逍遥法外。
