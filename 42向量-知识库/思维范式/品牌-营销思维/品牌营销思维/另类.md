**22. ADOPT ANOTHER CATEGORY’S STYLE****22.另类**

What is a cliché in one category may be surprisingly fresh in another.在一个类别中是陈词滥调的东西，在另一个类别中可能会出人意料地新鲜。

So if you find yourself in the automobile category, try speaking the language of sports.因此，如果你发现自己属于 "汽车族"，不妨试着说说体育语言。

Make corporate advertising feel like fashion advertising.让企业广告感觉像时尚广告。

Make travel advertising like cosmetic advertising.让旅游广告像化妆品广告一样。

Rework ads for fast moving consumer goods so they feel like ads for a social cause.重新制作快速消费品的广告，让它们感觉像是社会事业的广告。

Dress up a sports ad so it looks like a movie trailer.把体育广告打扮得像电影预告片。

Work a serious social message into an animated cartoon.在动画片中传递严肃的社会信息。

Turn a food commercial into a musical.将食品广告变成音乐剧

And remember, half-measures won’t do the trick. If you’re going for it, you have to go all the way.记住，半途而废是行不通的。如果你想成功，就必须全力以赴。
