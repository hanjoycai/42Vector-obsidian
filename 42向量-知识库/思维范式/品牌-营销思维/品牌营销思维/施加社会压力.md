**33. APPLY SOCIAL PRESSURE****33.施加社会压力**

Being social creatures, we are driven by and obsessed with what other people think of us.作为社会性动物，我们被其他人对我们的看法所驱使，并为之着迷。

We yearn to be liked, admired and respected by our peer group.我们渴望得到同龄人的喜欢、钦佩和尊重。

And the truth is that we will do almost anything to fit in.而事实是，为了融入社会，我们几乎不择手段。

Social pressure, therefore, is a powerful tool that can be used either to reinforce positive behavior (like volunteering with a charity) or to correct negative behavior (like quitting smoking).因此，社会压力是一种强大的工具，既可以用来强化积极的行为（比如为慈善机构提供志愿服务），也可以用来纠正消极的行为（比如戒烟）。

The pressure you apply can also be encouraging (a pat on the back and a *“Well done, you are awesome”*) or stigmatizing (*“Hey a**hole, what’s wrong with you?”*).你施加的压力可以是鼓励性的（拍拍后背，说 "干得好，你真棒"），也可以是鄙视性的（"嘿，混蛋，你怎么回事？）

With an encouraging approach, aim to create a new peer group of people with whom your target group can identify, a group that will help them fit in and hold them accountable for their actions.采用鼓励的方法，旨在创建一个目标群体能够认同的新同伴群体，这个群体将帮助他们融入其中，并让他们对自己的行为负责。

Think of peer coaches, real-life ‘buddy’ support systems and social-media support groups.想想同伴教练、现实生活中的 "伙伴 "支持系统和社交媒体支持小组。

Think of social media campaigns that use Facebook and Twitter, like the ones that encourage people to save the rainforest or donate their clothes for earthquake victims.想想使用 Facebook 和 Twitter 的社交媒体活动，比如鼓励人们拯救热带雨林或为地震灾民捐赠衣物的活动。

Alcoholics Anonymous is an example of an encouraging peer support group that aims to correct a negative behavior.匿名戒酒互助会就是一个旨在纠正消极行为的鼓励性同伴互助团体的例子。

If, however, you choose the stigmatizing approach, make sure that the stigma you create is targeted at the behavior and not the person behind it.但是，如果您选择污名化的方法，请确保您制造的污名针对的是行为，而不是行为背后的人。

Secondly, ensure that what you are trying to change is indeed a voluntary behavior and not the result of some medical condition that the person has no control over.其次，要确保你试图改变的确实是一种自愿行为，而不是当事人无法控制的某种疾病的结果。
