**20. CONNECT CAUSE WITH EFFECT****20.因果联系**

With social issues, often the biggest hurdle you face is that the benefit of what you are asking people to do is not immediately apparent.在社会问题上，你所面临的最大障碍往往是，你要求人们做的事情所带来的好处并不是立竿见影的。

For instance, a woman who turns off a couple of lights on Earth Day will never get to see the effect of her actions on the climate.例如，在地球日关掉几盏灯的妇女永远不会看到她的行为对气候的影响。

An office-goer who begins to recycle his plastic bottles won’t notice any significant difference in the size of the plastic patch in the oceans.一个开始回收塑料瓶的上班族不会注意到海洋中塑料斑块的大小有什么明显变化。

A smoker who quits is unlikely to notice any change in his lungs right away.戒烟者不太可能立即发现肺部有任何变化。

When cause and effect are separated by vast amounts of time and space, what can you do?当因果关系被巨大的时间和空间阻隔时，你能做些什么？

Well, you can simply bring them together visually.那么，你可以简单地将它们形象地组合在一起。

Juxtapose the two in the most interesting way you can think of.用你能想到的最有趣的方式将两者并列起来。
