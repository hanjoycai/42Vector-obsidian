**03. DRAMATIZE THE PROBLEM****03.将问题戏剧化**

Every product is a solution to a problem.每个产品都是解决问题的方案。

So it follows that the more interesting, urgent and serious the problem, the more impressive the solution.因此，问题越有趣、越紧迫、越严重，解决方案就越令人印象深刻。

For this approach, let the problem dominate the ad, taking up most of the space and time.对于这种方法，让问题主导广告，占据大部分篇幅和时间。

The ideal proportion is 90% problem and 10% solution.理想的比例是 90% 的问题和 10% 的解决方案。

When you think about it, that’s the way jokes are structured too — 90% set up and 10% punch line.仔细想想，笑话的结构也是如此--90% 的铺垫和 10% 的台词。

Begin by identifying all the problems that the product can solve and select the most insightful one from the list.首先找出产品能解决的所有问题，然后从中选出最有见地的一个。

Make sure the core of the problem is a truth, not a contrivance.确保问题的核心是事实，而不是臆造。

This will ensure that the audience can relate to it and imagine themselves in a similar situation.这将确保受众能够与之产生共鸣，并想象自己也会遇到类似的情况。

Having anchored yourself in a truth, let the problem fly.让自己立足于真理，让问题飞起来。

Find its most outrageous form.找到它最离谱的形式。

Throw the problem into interesting situations.将问题抛入有趣的情境中。

Thurst it on unusual people.把它放在不寻常的人身上。

Create a perfect storm for a character to battle.为角色创造一场完美的战斗风暴。

Then at the last minute, rescue that character from his predicament with your clever and punchy solution.然后在最后一刻，用你巧妙而有力的解决方案将人物从困境中解救出来。
