**31. OVERTURN PREJUDICE**** ****31.推翻偏见**

Find something about your category that people have a misconception about or are biased against and prove them wrong.找到人们对你的类别有误解或偏见的地方，并证明他们是错的。

Expose the prejudice and show that it was irrational and unfounded all along.揭露偏见，证明偏见一直以来都是不合理和毫无根据的。

Find the positive in the negative.在消极中寻找积极。

Reveal the beauty in what might, at first glance, seem ugly.让乍看起来丑陋的事物焕发出美丽的光彩。

Turn the weakest link into the strongest one.把最薄弱的环节变成最强的环节。

Kill a myth. 杀死一个神话

Slay a holy cow.杀死一头圣牛

Do all the things the world said could never be done.做所有世人都说不可能做到的事。
