**41. CONDUCT AN EXPERIMENT****41.进行实验**

Experiments, especially social ones, are now popular for the same reason reality television became popular.实验，尤其是社会实验，现在很流行，原因与真人秀节目流行的原因相同。

They appeal to our voyeuristic instincts.它们迎合了我们的窥视本能。

We get to compare ourselves with people who are dumped into situations that we may either wish we could be in, or are relieved that we are not.我们会把自己与那些陷入困境的人进行比较，而我们可能希望自己也能陷入困境，或者为自己没有陷入困境而感到欣慰。

For advertisers, they are a great option, because they are cheaper to stage and orchestrate than TV ads.对于广告商来说，它们是一个不错的选择，因为它们的舞台和策划成本都比电视广告低。

But in order for your audience to believe your experiment’s conclusions, it must be unbiased.但是，为了让受众相信你的实验结论，它必须不偏不倚。

And so when you conduct one, you have to follow some scientific principles.因此，在进行研究时，必须遵循一些科学原则。

Your experiment should start with the framing of a question or a hypothesis that you want to test.实验应从提出问题或假设开始。

Moreover, every participant must go through the same procedure.此外，每个参与者都必须经过相同的程序。

That doesn’t mean that you have to be serious, formal and dull.这并不意味着你必须严肃、正式和沉闷。

Your approach can as lighthearted and entertaining as your subject and your brand’s tone of voice will allow.在主题和品牌语气允许的范围内，你可以采取轻松娱乐的方式。

You can also take some liberties in the telling of the story.在讲述故事时，您也可以采取一些自由发挥的方式。

You may disclose to your viewers that it’s a branded experiment right up front. Or you may save it for a reveal right at the end.你可以事先向观众透露这是一个品牌实验。也可以留到最后揭晓。
