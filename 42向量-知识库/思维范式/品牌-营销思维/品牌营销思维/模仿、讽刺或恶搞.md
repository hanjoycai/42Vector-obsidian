**25. MAKE A PARODY, SATIRE OR SPOOF****25.模仿、讽刺或恶搞**

Being able to poke fun at authority is not just liberating, it’s often critical for effecting social change.能够嘲笑权威不仅是一种解放，而且往往对实现社会变革至关重要。

That’s why parody and satire have always been powerful, persuasive art forms.这就是为什么戏仿和讽刺一直是强大而有说服力的艺术形式。

So what’s the difference between parody and satire?那么，模仿和讽刺之间有什么区别呢？

Parody makes use of mimicry, while satire doesn’t.戏仿利用了模仿，而讽刺则没有。

Naturally, mocking the world is best left to brands with a sense of humour.当然，嘲讽世界最好还是留给有幽默感的品牌。

A brand that is able to laugh at itself is a likeable brand.能够自嘲的品牌才是讨人喜欢的品牌。
