**28. CHAMPION THE UNDERDOG****28.弱者冠军**

Consumers these days expect companies to have decent values and meaningful beliefs.如今，消费者希望企业拥有良好的价值观和有意义的信念。

In studies, they say what a company stands for will make a difference to whether or not they buy its products.在研究中，他们说一家公司所代表的理念会对他们是否购买其产品产生影响。

That being the case, a compelling way to sell a product is to convince the audience that the brand comes from a good place, that it is purpose-driven and has a magnanimous heart.在这种情况下，销售产品的一种令人信服的方式就是让受众相信，该品牌的出发点是好的，是以目标为导向的，是有宽广胸怀的。

Go champion the underdogs of society.去为社会中的弱势群体争光。

Stand up for the downtrodden.为受压迫者挺身而出。

Find victims and highlight their plight.寻找受害者，突出他们的困境。

Come up with a design or technology solution that helps the less-privileged.提出帮助弱势群体的设计或技术解决方案。

Offer your services for free and for as long as you can afford to.在力所能及的范围内免费提供服务。
