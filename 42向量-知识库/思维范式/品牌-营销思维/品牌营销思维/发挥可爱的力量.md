**35. USE THE POWER OF CUTE****35.发挥可爱的力量**

If you’re ever patted a puppy, watched a cat video on Facebook, or cooed over someone’s baby, then you are already familiar with the power of cute.如果你曾经拍过小狗，在 Facebook 上看过猫咪的视频，或者对别人家的宝宝嘘寒问暖，那么你就已经熟悉了可爱的力量。

‘Cute’ not only has the power to melt hearts, it can get people to endure hardship and expense.可爱 "不仅有融化人心的力量，还能让人们忍受艰辛和花费。

Ask any parent who has woken up in the middle of the night to change a nappy or comfort a colicky infant.试问有哪位父母曾在半夜醒来换尿布或安慰肠绞痛的婴儿。

Baby animals have just the right features to inspire us to care for them —big heads, large eyes, little noses and puffed cheeks.动物宝宝的头大、眼睛大、鼻子小、脸颊鼓鼓，这些特征恰好能激发我们对它们的关爱。

This is why WWF uses a panda as their logo, and not, for instance, the endangered Chinese giant salamander.这就是为什么世界自然基金会使用熊猫作为他们的标志，而不是濒临灭绝的中国大鲵。

This is why Hello Kitty and Mickey Mouse rake in billions of dollars.这也是 Hello Kitty 和米老鼠赚取数十亿美元的原因。

And this is why ‘baby-face’ cars like the Mini, the Beetle and the Fiat 500 are so popular.这也是迷你、甲壳虫和菲亚特 500 等 "娃娃脸 "汽车大受欢迎的原因。

In Japan, cuteness (*kawaii*) is everywhere, and Japanese businesses, big and small, use ‘cute’ to sell their products.在日本，可爱（卡哇伊）无处不在，日本的大小企业都利用 "可爱 "来推销自己的产品。

Even the Japanese police market themselves with a cute mascot.就连日本警察也用可爱的吉祥物来推销自己。
