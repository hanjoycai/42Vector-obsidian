**38. CUSTOMIZE AND PERSONALIZE****38.定制和个性化**

No one likes to be made to feel like a number.没有人喜欢被当成一个数字。

Nobody wants to be treated as just another face in a crowd.没有人愿意被当作人群中的另一张面孔。

But until now, large companies couldn’t help but treat their customers that way.但在此之前，大公司无法不这样对待他们的客户。

Today, thanks to advances in technology, they can make every member of their audience feel as if their brand exists exclusively for them.如今，由于技术的进步，他们可以让每一位受众都感觉到自己的品牌是专为他们而存在的。

So think of a way to tailor your product, your message or your experience for every individual who views it.因此，要想办法为每个浏览者量身定制产品、信息或体验。

The more customized the experience, the more flattering it is.越是定制化的体验，越能让人受宠若惊。
