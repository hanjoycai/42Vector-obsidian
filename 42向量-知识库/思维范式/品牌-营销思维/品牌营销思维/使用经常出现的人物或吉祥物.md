**15. USE A RECURRING CHARACTER OR MASCOT****15.使用经常出现的人物或吉祥物**

Create a fictional character who will be the spokesperson for your brand and will feature in every piece of communication.创建一个虚构人物，让他成为你的品牌代言人，并出现在你的每一次传播中。

Whether it’s a talking animal, a cartoon monster or a human actor with super powers, it needs to be memorable.无论是会说话的动物、卡通怪物，还是拥有超能力的人类演员，都需要让人过目难忘。

Not just in looks but in its personality, mannerism and behavior.不仅是外观，还有个性、举止和行为。

Mascots have been hugely successful in advertising because they’ve allowed large companies to speak in one voice and present a single, consistent human face to the crowds.吉祥物在广告中取得了巨大成功，因为它们让大公司能够用一个声音说话，并向人们展示一个单一、一致的人的面孔。

It’s simply easier for the public to bond with a talking gecko or a two-dimensional duck than a troupe of nameless, faceless, characterless corporate executives.对公众来说，与一群无名、无脸、无性格的企业高管相比，一只会说话的壁虎或一只二维的鸭子更容易让他们产生共鸣。

Besides, those executives age, wrinkle and expand over time. A mascot, on the other hand, keeps its good looks forever.此外，随着时间的推移，这些高管会老化、起皱和膨胀。而吉祥物却能永葆青春。

A mascot can also speak to people of all ages, races and cultures.吉祥物还能与不同年龄、种族和文化的人交流。

And a mascot is scalable. It has value beyond a single ad.吉祥物是可扩展的。它的价值超越了单个广告。
