**48. CONDUCT A PRODUCT TRIAL****48.进行产品试验**

Free trials have been around since the beginning of business.免费试用从创业之初就开始了。

Indeed, everyone knows that for a new product, free trials are a good way to recruit customers.事实上，每个人都知道，对于新产品来说，免费试用是招徕顾客的好方法。

But what if there’s nothing new about your product?但是，如果你的产品没有任何新意呢？

Just get people who are not part of your target audience to try it for free.只要让不属于目标受众的人免费试用就可以了。

For instance, if you’re selling tea, offer it to people who only drink coffee.例如，如果你卖的是茶，那么就把茶提供给只喝咖啡的人。

If you’re selling a truck, let sports car drivers take a test drive.如果你要卖一辆卡车，让跑车司机试驾一下。

If you’re marketing a resort, offer a free holiday to people who have never taken one.如果你在推销度假村，可以向从未享受过度假的人提供免费假期。

Of course, you can’t expect to make instant converts of the new group.当然，你不能指望让新群体立即皈依你。

But the resulting film might just be entertaining enough to create buzz on social media.不过，这部影片的娱乐性可能足以在社交媒体上引起轰动。

And this fresh look at a familiar experience will reassure your core consumers that your product is still a treat.以全新的面貌呈现熟悉的体验，会让你的核心消费者确信，你的产品仍然是一种享受。
