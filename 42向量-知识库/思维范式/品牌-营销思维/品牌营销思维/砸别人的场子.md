**39. CRASH SOMEONE ELSE’S PARTY****39.砸别人的场子**

Let’s be honest. Your audience has better things to do than watch your ad.实话实说吧。你的受众有比看你的广告更重要的事情要做。

After all, there’s a world of movies, TV, gossip and news out there.毕竟，外面有一个电影、电视、八卦和新闻的世界。

So identify other subjects related to yours that are currently trending on social media, pick the one with the most interesting connection to yours and hijack the discussion.因此，找出当前社交媒体上流行的与你的主题相关的其他话题，挑选与你的主题最有关联的话题进行讨论。

If you do it intelligently, tastefully and with consideration, you will get not just people’s attention but also their appreciation.如果你做得聪明、有品位、考虑周到，你不仅会得到人们的关注，还会得到他们的赞赏。

The trick is in respecting your consumer.诀窍在于尊重消费者。

And that means making them feel rewarded, not cheated.这就意味着要让他们觉得自己得到了回报，而不是被欺骗。

As Bob Thacker (Senior Marketing Officer at OfficeMax) once said, “*All advertising is unwanted. So if you’re going to crash the party, bring some champagne with you.”*Bob Thacker（OfficeMax 高级营销官）曾经说过："所有的广告都是不受欢迎的。所以，如果你要去参加派对，就带点香槟来吧。
