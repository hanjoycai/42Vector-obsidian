**23. MAKE THE FAMILIAR UNFAMILIAR 化陌生为熟悉**

Sometimes, the execution is the idea.有时，执行就是理念。

You can make something old feel fresh and new just by treating it differently.你可以通过不同的方式让旧事物焕然一新。

Change the setting. 更改设置。

Rotate the actors. 旋转演员。

Play with the scale. 玩转音阶

Make the large things small and the small things large.大事化小，小事化了。

Go from the outside looking in to the inside looking out.从外面往里看，到里面往外看。

Speed things up and slow them down.加速和减速

Switch from a bird’s eye view to an ant’s eye view.从鸟瞰视角切换到蚁视视角。

Reprogram the sounds, the voices and the music.重新设置声音、语音和音乐。

Make the invisible visible.让无形变得有形。

Swap the colours. 交换颜色

And peek through a different lens.并通过不同的镜头进行窥探。

Keep going until you arrive at something that’s never been done before.一直走下去，直到取得前所未有的成就。
