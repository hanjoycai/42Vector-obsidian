**47. PARTNER WITH ANOTHER BRAND****47.与其他品牌合作**

Think of other products, services or people that you could tie in with.想一想您可以与之联系的其他产品、服务或人员。

Tie-ins not only save on costs, but they also give all the brands involved more eyeballs than they would get on their own.捆绑销售不仅能节省成本，还能为所有相关品牌带来比单打独斗更多的眼球效应。

The trick to a successful tie-in is to find things that go together like peanut butter and jelly.成功搭配的诀窍在于找到像花生酱和果冻一样搭配的东西。

A coffee brand could tie-in with a music store or a bookstore.咖啡品牌可以与音乐商店或书店合作。

A computer hardware brand could tie-in with a software brand.电脑硬件品牌可以与软件品牌结合。

A luxury car brand could tie in with a brand that sells premium luggage or golf clubs.豪华汽车品牌可以与销售高档箱包或高尔夫球杆的品牌合作。

A real-estate company could tie-in with a storage company.房地产公司可以与仓储公司合作。

Both brands need to have the same goals, the same audience and preferably the same method of distribution, so everybody has a sweet time and gets to the podium.两个品牌需要有相同的目标、相同的受众，最好还有相同的分销方式，这样每个人都能享受甜蜜时光，登上领奖台。
