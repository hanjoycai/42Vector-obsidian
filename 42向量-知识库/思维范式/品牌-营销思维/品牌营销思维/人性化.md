**24. HUMANIZE**** ****24.人性化**

If you’re stuck trying to communicate an abstract concept, try giving it a human dimension.如果你在试图传达一个抽象概念时遇到困难，不妨试着赋予它人性的一面。

Call us humans biased. But the more something looks and sounds like us, the more we are able to relate to it.这可以说是我们人类的偏见。但越是看起来和听起来像我们的东西，我们就越能与之产生共鸣。

So try personifying your subject.因此，试着将主题拟人化。

Give it a human name, shape and voice.给它一个人类的名字、形状和声音。

Don’t stop there. Give it a human trait, personality and feelings too.不要止步于此。还要赋予它人性、个性和情感。

Remember that every culture and civilization in history fashioned their Gods in their own likeness.请记住，历史上的每一种文化和文明都是按照自己的样子塑造神灵的。

Mickey the mouse, Barney the dinosaur, and Nemo the fish were anthropomorphic for a reason.老鼠米奇、恐龙巴尼和小鱼尼莫拟人化是有原因的。

Technology is acquiring human qualities too. IBM’s Watson and Apple’s Siri hint at its human future.科技也在获取人类的特质。IBM 的沃森（Watson）和苹果的 Siri 都暗示了人类的未来。
