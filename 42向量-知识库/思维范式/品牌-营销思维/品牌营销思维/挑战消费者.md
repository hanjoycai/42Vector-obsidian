**19. CHALLENGE THE CONSUMER****19.挑战消费者**

Engage your audience by setting them a challenge.通过向受众提出挑战来吸引他们。

Make it a positive, inspiring one while you’re at it, a goal worth pursuing.让它成为一个积极的、鼓舞人心的、值得追求的目标。

It should not only make them feel good about the brand, but about themselves too.这不仅要让他们对品牌产生好感，也要让他们对自己产生好感。

Ensure that the whole experience is fresh, fun and easy to share.确保整个体验新鲜、有趣、易于分享。

Everyone who participates should go home with a sense of achievement, a great story and some lifelong memories.每个参与者都应该带着成就感、精彩的故事和终生难忘的回忆回家。

And what about the people who didn’t participate or only stumbled upon the video later online?那么那些没有参与或只是后来偶然在网上看到视频的人呢？

Tell the story in a way that makes them feel rewarded too, for the time they spent on it.在讲述故事的过程中，也要让他们感觉到自己在故事上花费的时间得到了回报。
