**51. DRESS UP AS NEWS OR ENTERTAINMENT51.假扮新闻**

The truth is that people don’t like ads.事实上，人们并不喜欢广告。

So try and get under their ad-radar by making your ad look as little like an ad as possible.因此，请尽量让您的广告看起来不像广告，从而避开他们的广告雷达。

Package it as a home video, a documentary film, a music video, a gif, a television program, a magazine article, a news report or a Facebook post.将其包装成家庭视频、纪录片、音乐视频、gif、电视节目、杂志文章、新闻报道或 Facebook 帖子。

It’s sly, for sure. One could even argue that it’s evil.这确实很狡猾。甚至可以说它是邪恶的。

But if you do it subtly, your audience won’t resent having been tricked into spending time with a commercial message.但如果你做得巧妙，受众就不会反感被商业信息欺骗而花费时间。

And if it’s truly entertaining, funny or informative, they might even share it with their friends. You never know.如果内容确实有趣、好玩或信息量大，他们甚至会与朋友分享。你永远不会知道。
