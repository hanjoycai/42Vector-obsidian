**18. CHALLENGE YOURSELF 挑战自我**

Force your brand off its couch by setting it a challenge.给品牌设置挑战，让它从沙发上站起来。

And don’t make the challenge easy either.也不要让挑战变得简单。

Select a goal that has never been attempted before.选择一个从未尝试过的目标。

And announce it to the world.并向全世界宣布。

Then go after that goal with determination.然后坚定不移地朝着目标前进。

The pursuit should make a compelling story.追求应该是一个引人入胜的故事。

Even if the brand doesn’t succeed in attaining the goal, it still wins.即使品牌没有成功实现目标，它仍然是赢家。

Because a brand that frequently sets the bar higher will be noticed, respected and admired.因为一个经常提高标准的品牌会受到关注、尊重和钦佩。
