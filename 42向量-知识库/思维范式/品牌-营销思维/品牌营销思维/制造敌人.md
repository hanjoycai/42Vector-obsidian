**26. MAKE AN ENEMY****26.制造敌人**

Many much-loved brands got that way by publicly identifying their enemies.许多备受喜爱的品牌都是通过公开指认自己的敌人才获得了这样的地位。

Why would that be so?为什么会这样呢？

Because it’s often easier to articulate a brand’s values by talking about what it stands against than what it stands for.因为要阐述一个品牌的价值，谈论它反对什么往往比谈论它代表什么更容易。

And if you want to bond people in a group and rally them to a cause, the quickest way is to name an adversary.如果你想把一个团体中的人团结起来，让他们为某项事业奋斗，最快捷的方法就是说出一个对手的名字。

Just look at sports fans when their team is up against a rival.看看体育迷们在球队对阵对手时的表现就知道了。

This doesn’t mean, however, that your enemy has to be a competitive brand.但这并不意味着你的敌人必须是一个具有竞争力的品牌。

In fact, it might be better if the enemy is an idea or a belief.事实上，如果敌人是一种想法或信念，可能会更好。

Politicians and religious groups understand this and use it all the time.政治家和宗教团体都明白这一点，并一直在利用这一点。

So there’s no reason why brands shouldn’t.因此，品牌没有理由不这样做。

The corollary to this is that you can make an idea really attractive to one group of people by showing them that another group hates it.由此得出的推论是，你可以通过向一群人展示另一群人对某一想法的憎恶，使这一想法对他们产生真正的吸引力。

Remember that hate is a powerful thing.记住，仇恨是一种强大的东西。

And for an audience watching from the sidelines, it’s definitely more entertaining than love.而对于在一旁观看的观众来说，这无疑比爱情更具娱乐性。
