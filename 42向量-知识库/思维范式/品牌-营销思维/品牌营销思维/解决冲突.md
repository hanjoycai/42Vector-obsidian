**29. RESOLVE CONFLICTS**** ****29.解决冲突**

The world is full of clashes, big and small.世界充满了大大小小的冲突。

Everywhere you look, you find people who don’t get along.无论你走到哪里，你都会发现有不和的人。

And there lies the opportunity for your brand.这就是品牌的机遇所在。

Identify unresolved conflicts—between family members, employees, neighbors, teams, generations, communities, societies, races, and nations — and find a relevant way for your brand to bring the two warring parties together.找出未解决的冲突--家庭成员、员工、邻居、团队、世代、社区、社会、种族和国家之间的冲突--并为您的品牌找到一种相关的方式，将交战双方凝聚在一起。

Your efforts should end in mutual appreciation, respect and acceptance.你们的努力应该以相互欣赏、尊重和接受而告终。

And the resulting story should end with a statement about your brand’s idealistic view of the world.在故事的结尾处，应阐述品牌的理想主义世界观。
