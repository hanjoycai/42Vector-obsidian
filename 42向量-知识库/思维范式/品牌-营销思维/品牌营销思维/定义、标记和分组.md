**13. DEFINE, LABEL AND GROUP****13.定义、标记和分组**

As humans, we have this need to identify and classify the world around us.作为人类，我们需要对周围的世界进行识别和分类。

We are compelled to name things and sort them into boxes.我们不得不为事物命名，并将它们分类装箱。

We do this to everything—from flowers and clouds to thoughts and actions.我们对任何事物都是如此，从花朵和云朵到思想和行动。

We are prone to see groupings even where there aren’t any, since that’s the way our brains make sense of the world.即使没有分组，我们也很容易看到分组，因为这是我们大脑认识世界的方式。

Put this tendency to work.将这种趋势付诸实践。

Coin an evocative name or symbol for something you want to encourage.为你想要鼓励的事物起一个令人回味的名字或符号。

Conversely, find a distasteful name for something you want to discourage.反之，为你想阻止的事情取一个令人讨厌的名字。

Labeling draws attention to whatever’s been labeled.贴标签会引起人们对标签内容的关注。

Labels can make one group feel exclusive and another group feel excluded.标签会让一个群体感到被排斥，另一个群体感到被排斥。

By switching labels, we can also change how something is perceived.通过更换标签，我们也可以改变人们对事物的看法。

And over time, we can change people’s behavior too.随着时间的推移，我们也能改变人们的行为。
