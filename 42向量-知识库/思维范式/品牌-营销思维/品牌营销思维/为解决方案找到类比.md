**07. FIND AN ANALOGY FOR THE SOLUTION07.为解决方案找到类比**

Some products have benefits that are complex, uninteresting, or even socially unacceptable to talk about.有些产品的优点很复杂、无趣，甚至不被社会所接受。

In such instances, explore the analogy approach.在这种情况下，应探索类比方法。

Although it can be argued that using an analogy is borrowing interest from another topic, it is a useful tool.虽然可以说使用类比是从另一个话题中借用兴趣，但它是一个有用的工具。

In many cases, it might even be the only effective tool.在许多情况下，它甚至可能是唯一有效的工具。

An analogy can infuse a boring product benefit with much needed emotion and memorability.一个比喻可以为枯燥的产品利益注入急需的情感和记忆力。

As with the previous tool, remember that the success of this approach depends on the word, phrase or line that links the analogy with your product.与前一种工具一样，请记住，这种方法的成功取决于将类比与您的产品联系起来的单词、短语或台词。
