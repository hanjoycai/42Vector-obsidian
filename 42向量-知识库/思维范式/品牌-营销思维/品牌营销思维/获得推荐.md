**14. GET A TESTIMONIAL****14.获得推荐信**

Testimonials lend credibility to your brand message.推荐信可为您的品牌信息增添可信度。

But who will make you message more credible? Customers, experts or celebrities?但谁会让你的信息更可信呢？客户、专家还是名人？

Customer testimonials can be quite persuasive, provided that they appear to be unbiased.只要客户的评价不带偏见，就会很有说服力。

You could get loyal customers to tell the world why they’ve stayed with your brand, or new customers to talk about why they switched over.您可以让忠实客户告诉世界他们为什么一直使用您的品牌，或者让新客户讲述他们为什么转而使用您的品牌。

With expert testimonials, such as from lawyers, scientists, doctors and engineers, the persuasive power comes from their skills, talents, knowledge and reputation in the field.专家推荐，如律师、科学家、医生和工程师的推荐，其说服力来自他们的技能、才能、知识和在该领域的声誉。

Celebrity testimonials, by far the most popular option, give you immediate awareness, recognition and interest.名人推荐是迄今为止最受欢迎的选择，它能让你立即获得知名度、认可度和兴趣。

But why do people pay so much attention to celebrities?但为什么人们会如此关注名人呢？

It could be that we feel we know them intimately, having seen them on screens so often.这可能是因为我们经常在屏幕上看到他们，觉得自己与他们非常熟悉。

Research shows that when people are outstanding in one field, this creates a halo effect.研究表明，当一个人在某一领域表现突出时，就会产生光环效应。

We expect the celebrities we admire to be intelligent, moral, reputable and universally awesome in other fields too.我们希望自己崇拜的名人也能聪明、有道德、有声望，而且在其他领域也是众望所归。

On the flipside, there is always the danger with using celebrities that at the end of the day, viewers only remember the celebrity and just can’t recall the brand.反过来说，使用名人总有一个危险，那就是到最后，观众只记得名人，却想不起品牌。

And when the celebrity fails to live up to our moral expectations (as happened with Tiger Woods and Lance Armstrong), the brand image suffers.而当名人辜负了我们的道德期望时（就像泰格-伍兹和兰斯-阿姆斯特朗那样），品牌形象就会受到损害。

The creative approach to using testimonials is to start with the most unexpected person you can imagine.使用推荐信的创新方法是从你能想象到的最意想不到的人开始。

Wait. It doesn’t even have to be a person.等等，甚至不需要是一个人。
