**04. DRAMATIZE THE SOLUTION****04.将解决方案戏剧化**

In this instance, you are going to lead with a solution instead of a problem.在这种情况下，你要以解决方案而不是问题作为引导。

90% of your ad will be an interesting, dramatic, ‘WTF?’ situation that results from someone using your product.你的广告 90% 都会是一个有趣的、戏剧性的、"WTF? "的情境，而这个情境正是某人使用你的产品的结果。

The viewer is not told how this situation came about until the very end.直到最后，观众才知道这种情况是如何产生的。

The last few seconds of your ad are for a clever connecting phrase that reveal that your product was behind it all.广告的最后几秒钟是一个巧妙的连接词，揭示你的产品是这一切的幕后推手。

So where do you begin?那么，从哪里开始呢？

You start by figuring out that connecting phrase.首先要找出连接词。

Write down as many interesting expressions of your product’s benefit as you can and pick the one that seems the most insightful, fresh and true.尽可能多地写下对产品优点的有趣表述，然后挑选出最有见地、最新鲜、最真实的表述。

Craft this phrase till it shines.精雕细琢，让这句话熠熠生辉。

It’s the hinge that opens the door between your product and amazing work.它是打开你的产品和令人惊叹的作品之间大门的铰链。

The more interesting this phrase, the more memorable the situation up front.这句话越有趣，前面的情况就越令人难忘。
