**10. CREATE FANTASY WORLDS, PEOPLE AND THINGS****10.创造奇幻的世界、人和事物**

Invent a world that obeys not the existing laws of physics but your own made-up rules.创造一个不遵守现有物理定律，而是遵守你自己编造的规则的世界。

In other words, play God.换句话说，就是扮演上帝。

Cook up talking animals and flying men.烹饪会说话的动物和会飞的人。

Paint the sky purple and the oceans pink.把天空涂成紫色，把海洋涂成粉色。

Wish into existence a garden of Eden or a hell on earth.希望有一个伊甸园或人间地狱。

Try out superheroes and fairies, giants and bionic men.尝试超级英雄和仙女、巨人和仿生人。

Anything goes as long as it leads to the product benefit.只要能带来产品效益，什么都可以。
