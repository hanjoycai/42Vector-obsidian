**27. USE THE POWER OF GUILT****27.运用内疚的力量**

Guilt is a strong emotion.内疚是一种强烈的情绪。

And non-profit organizations use it all the time to get people to part with cash.非营利组织也经常用它来吸引人们拿出现金。

But it works for brands in other industries too.但它也适用于其他行业的品牌。

You can make the consumer feel guilty for a number of things —overspending, overindulging, smoking, texting, not exercising, not being patriotic, not spending enough time with their kids, being homophobic or misogynistic, being prejudiced against other cultures…you name it.你可以让消费者对很多事情产生负罪感--过度消费、过度放纵、吸烟、发短信、不锻炼、不爱国、没有足够的时间陪伴孩子、仇视同性恋或厌恶女性、对其他文化有偏见......你说得出来。

We carry guilt as individuals.作为个人，我们背负着罪恶感。

And we carry it as a society too, for the way we collectively treat women, animals and the less privileged groups among us.作为一个社会，我们对待妇女、动物和弱势群体的方式也是如此。

So all you might need to do as a marketer is rekindle that existing guilt and offer your solution.因此，作为营销人员，你所要做的可能就是重新点燃现有的负罪感，并提供你的解决方案。

Notice, too, that there’s a stronger motivation for people to do the right thing when they feel publicly exposed and watched.还要注意的是，当人们感到自己被公开曝光并受到监视时，他们会有更强的动力去做正确的事。

But try not to rub their faces in that guilt. Usually, a subtle touch works best.但尽量不要让他们感到内疚。通常，含蓄的抚慰效果最好。
