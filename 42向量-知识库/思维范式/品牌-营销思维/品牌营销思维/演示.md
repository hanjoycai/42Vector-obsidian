**11. DEMONSTRATE**** ****11.演示**

Seeing will always be believing.眼见为实。

That’s why the ‘demo’ has been a staple of advertising since its early days.这就是为什么 "演示 "从一开始就是广告的主打。

But if you think a ‘demo’ stops at scientists in lab coats, liquids in beakers and graphics of molecules, think again.但是，如果你认为 "演示 "仅仅停留在科学家穿着白大褂、烧杯中的液体和分子图形上，那就大错特错了。

Showing how a product works is just one kind of ‘demo’ and there are many more options for you to choose from.展示产品如何工作只是 "演示 "的一种，还有更多选项供您选择。

You can demonstrate, for example, the consequences of not using the product.例如，您可以演示不使用产品的后果。

You can demonstrate how to live the lifestyle associated with the product.您可以展示如何过与产品相关的生活方式。

You can demonstrate a side effect of using the product.您可以展示使用该产品的副作用。

You can even demonstrate how to use a complementary product.您甚至可以演示如何使用配套产品。

And the tone of your demo doesn’t have to be serious either. It can be fun. It can be satirical. It can even be interactive.演示的语气也不一定要严肃。它可以很有趣。可以是讽刺性的。甚至可以是互动的。

But it’s good to structure it so it ends with a satisfying “Aha!”.不过，最好还是把它安排好，让它以一个令人满意的 "啊哈！"结束。
