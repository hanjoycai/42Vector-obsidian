**12. TAKE THE AUDIENCE BACKSTAGE****12.带领观众回到后台**

Show your viewers how your product is made.

向观众展示产品的生产过程。

Show them what makes it special.向他们展示它的特别之处。

Take them to the fields and the streams where your ingredients come from.带他们去田野和溪流看看，那里才是你的食材之源。

Take them on a tour of the processing plant, the assembly line, the workshop, the office and the artist’s studio.带他们参观加工厂、装配线、车间、办公室和艺术家工作室。

Introduce them to the farmers, the miners, the workforce, the sales force, the big boss, his family and his assistants.向他们介绍农民、矿工、工人、销售人员、大老板、他的家人和助手。

Let them see the passion with which it is produced and the sacrifices to make it great.让他们看到制作的激情，以及为使其伟大而做出的牺牲。

Let them hear the stories right from the makers’ mouths.让他们直接聆听制作者口中的故事。

Your backstage doesn’t have to be real either. It can be totally made-up.你的后台也不一定是真实的。它可以是完全虚构的。

Sometimes the truth is more interesting than fiction. Sometimes it’s not.有时，真相比虚构更有趣。有时则不然。
