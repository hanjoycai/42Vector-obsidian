**37. INVENT A COMPLEMENTARY PRODUCT****37.发明补充产品**

Think of a new product that would perfectly complement your existing one while adding value to the brand.想一个既能完美补充现有产品，又能增加品牌价值的新产品。

It could be a smart social-device that is able to share information with other products or connect customers with each other.它可以是一个智能社交设备，能够与其他产品共享信息，或将客户彼此联系起来。

Launching a complimentary product will make the brand newsworthy again.推出赠品将使品牌再次成为新闻。

When you advertise the new product, you naturally advertise the brand.在宣传新产品的同时，自然也就宣传了品牌。

It also establishes credibility in the industry and attracts customers to the website.它还能建立行业信誉，吸引客户访问网站。

If a full investment in a new product is too daunting, consider launching it with just a limited quantity in a small test market.如果对新产品进行全额投资过于艰巨，可以考虑在小规模测试市场上限量推出。
