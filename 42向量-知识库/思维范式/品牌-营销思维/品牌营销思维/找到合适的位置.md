**49. FIND A FITTING LOCATION****49.找到合适的位置**

Not so long ago, when ad people talked about ‘media’, you could be pretty sure they were referring to only print, radio, billboards or television.不久前，当广告人谈论 "媒体 "时，你可以肯定他们指的只是印刷品、广播、广告牌或电视。

That’s not true anymore.现在已经不是这样了。

Thanks to the internet, social media and advancements in digital technology, any surface at any location can now be used to send a message.得益于互联网、社交媒体和数字技术的进步，现在任何地点的任何表面都可以用来发送信息。

Just by picking the right location to deliver your message, you can be topical, relevant and interesting.只需选择合适的地点传递信息，就能使信息具有时事性、相关性和趣味性。

You can be in the exact spot where you appear the most dramatic, competitive and brilliant.你可以在最引人注目、最具竞争力和最出色的地方出现。

You can be invisible when you’re not needed and visible only when you are.你可以在不需要你的时候隐身，只有在需要你的时候才显身。

You can be right in people’s faces or deep inside their pockets.你可以当着人们的面，也可以深入他们的口袋。
