**21. USE A UNIQUE ATTRIBUTE****21.使用唯一属性**

Does your product have something that makes it stand out from the crowd?您的产品是否与众不同？

Does it have a unique name, logo, color, shape or feature?它是否有独特的名称、标识、颜色、形状或特征？

Does it have an unusual history?它有不寻常的历史吗？

Is there something special about where it comes from? Is it the only wristwatch made in Greece or the only beer brewed in Papua New Guinea?它的产地有什么特别之处吗？是希腊制造的唯一腕表，还是巴布亚新几内亚酿造的唯一啤酒？

Is there something different in its ingredients, in the way it is processed, or in the way it is used?它的成分、加工方式或使用方法是否有什么不同？

Is there a great story associated with the founder, like with Bill Gates for Microsoft, or Richard Branson for Virgin?是否有与创始人相关的伟大故事，如微软的比尔-盖茨或维珍的理查德-布兰森？

If you can’t find anything worth talking about, invent something.如果找不到值得谈论的话题，那就发明点什么。

Or reposition and reframe an existing characteristic so it stands out.或者重新定位和重塑现有特征，使其脱颖而出。
