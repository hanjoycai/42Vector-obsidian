**17. SWAP ROLES**** ****17.角色互换**

Here you’ll take comparison to the extreme.在这里，你将把比较发挥到极致。

Identify two things (or groups of people) that you think should be compared and literally get them to switch places.找出你认为应该进行比较的两件事情（或两组人），并让它们互换位置。

A privileged group, when forced to walk in the shoes of an underprivileged one, will quickly cultivate empathy and appreciation for them, besides seeing the error of their own ways.当一个特权群体被迫站在弱势群体的立场上时，除了看到自己的错误之外，还会很快培养出对他们的同情和感激。

That’s why this is such an useful tool in cause advertising.这就是为什么这在公益广告中是如此有用的工具。
