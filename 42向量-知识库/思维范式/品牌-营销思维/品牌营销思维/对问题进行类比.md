**06. FIND AN ANALOGY FOR THE PROBLEM****06.对问题进行类比**

This approach is especially useful when the problem that your brand solves is unfamiliar, uninteresting, complex, controversial or even unmentionable

当你的品牌所要解决的问题面临陌生、无趣、复杂、有争议，甚至难以启齿时，这种方法尤其有用。

Cue the analogy. 打比方。

Analogies work because our brains use patterns and shortcuts all the time to understand and deal with the world.类比之所以有效，是因为我们的大脑一直在使用模式和捷径来理解和处理这个世界。

With the right analogy, you can make that problem feel fresh, relevant, comprehensible or socially acceptable.有了正确的类比，你就能让问题变得新鲜、贴切、易于理解或为社会所接受。

The key to success is getting the connecting line just right, the one that links the analogy problem with the real world problem you want to highlight.成功的关键在于恰到好处的连接线，即把类比问题与你想要强调的现实世界问题联系起来的连接线。
