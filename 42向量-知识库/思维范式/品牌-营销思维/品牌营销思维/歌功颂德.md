**01. GLORIFY AND CELEBRATE****01.歌功颂德**

We all share this deep desire to belong to something greater than ourselves.

我们都有这种深深的渴望，希望属于比自己更伟大的东西。

That’s why we have religions, heroes, nations, tribes, clubs, teams, sides, communities and political parties.这就是为什么我们有宗教、英雄、国家、部落、俱乐部、团队、阵营、社区和政党。

Take advantage of this basic human need. Find something bigger than your product and put it on a pedestal.

利用人类的这一基本需求。找到比你的产品更重要的东西，并把它放在一个基座上。

You could celebrate something intangible like a spirit, a human quality, an attitude, a way of living or a school of thought.

你可以颂扬一些无形的东西，比如一种精神、一种人类品质、一种态度、一种生活方式或一种思想流派。

You could eulogize an individual or a group of people.你可以讴歌一个人，也可以讴歌一群人。

You could glorify an object or a place.

你可以美化一件物品或一个地方。

You could even heap praise on a particular era, a golden age, a journey through time or a historic legacy.

你甚至可以赞美一个特定的时代、一个黄金时代、一段穿越时空的旅程或一份历史遗产。

But at all costs, avoid the temptation to put your own brand on that pedestal.但无论如何，

都要避免将自己的品牌推上神坛。

Be the priest, not the deity.

做牧师，而不是神。

No brand should glorify itself. After all, no one likes a narcissist.

任何品牌都不应美化自己。毕竟，没人喜欢自恋狂。

Instead, be the means through which customers can connect with a bigger and far more sacred ideal.相反，要成为客户与更大、更神圣的理想建立联系的途径。
