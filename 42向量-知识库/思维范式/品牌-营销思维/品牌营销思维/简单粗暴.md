**36. BE BRUTALLY SIMPLE****36.简单粗暴**

Try to come up with the simplest expression of your proposition.试着用最简单的方式表达你的主张。

Impose restrictions on yourself and eliminate everything that’s unnecessary.对自己施加限制，消除一切不必要的东西。

Ask yourself how you would cope if you had to execute your idea with very little money.问问自己，如果你不得不用很少的钱来实现你的想法，你会如何应对。

What if you could use only a single locked-off camera, just one actor, or just one location?如果只能使用一台锁定的摄像机、一名演员或一个拍摄地点呢？

What if you could use no words at all?如果你可以完全不说话呢？

What if you weren’t allowed to use images and had to convey your message in just words?如果不允许使用图片，而只能用文字来传达信息呢？

What if you had only 10 seconds or less to say your piece?如果您只有 10 秒或更短的时间发表自己的观点呢？

It’s often the case that the greater the limitations, the more distilled the idea.通常情况下，限制越多，想法就越精炼。
