**32. BE BRUTALLY HONEST****32.开诚布公**

Tell the absolute truth about your brand and your product.绝对真实地介绍自己的品牌和产品。

Or even your category and its consumers.甚至是您所在的类别及其消费者。

Be at pains to point out imperfections and shortcomings.不厌其烦地指出不完美和不足之处。

Admit that mistakes were made and promises were broken.承认犯过错误，违背过承诺。

Apologize for having failed to provide absolute satisfaction.对未能提供绝对满意的服务表示歉意。

This approach can be very disarming because consumers expect advertising to be full of boast.这种做法会让人很不舒服，因为消费者希望广告充满夸夸其谈的成分。

So when you admit to your weaknesses—as an individual or even as a company—it can be refreshing.因此，当你承认自己的弱点时--作为一个人，甚至作为一个公司--会让人耳目一新。

It will also make you more credible when you do come around to pointing out your strengths.这也会让你在指出自己的优势时更加可信。
