**05. LET THE SOLUTION CREATE A NEW PROBLEM****05.让解决方案产生新问题**

One way to make your product benefit dramatic is to let it create an entirely new problem for the user.让产品产生巨大效益的方法之一，就是让产品为用户创造一个全新的问题。

Winning the lottery, for instance, might lead to the problem of figuring out which of your new friends are true friends.比如说，中了彩票可能会带来一个问题，那就是弄清哪些新朋友是真正的朋友。

Whiter, brighter clothes — the result of using a new washing detergent— could lead to the new problem of being swarmed by moths at night.由于使用了新的洗涤剂，衣服变得更白、更亮，这可能会导致晚上飞蛾成群的新问题。

Buying a higher definition TV might result in a family communicating even less with each other.购买更高清晰度的电视机可能会导致家庭之间的沟通更少。

What makes this approach work is the exaggeration of the new problem to such an extent that viewers don’t really see it as negative, but as an entertaining twist.这种方法的成功之处在于对新问题的夸大，以至于观众并没有真正将其视为负面问题，而是将其视为一种娱乐性的转折。

At any rate, the admission of a ‘negative’, especially a harmless one, is an interesting technique in persuasion, because it makes the brand appear more honest, approachable and human.无论如何，承认 "负面"，尤其是无害的 "负面"，是一种有趣的说服技巧，因为它能让品牌显得更加诚实、平易近人和人性化。
