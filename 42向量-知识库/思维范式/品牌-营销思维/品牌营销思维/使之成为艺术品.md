**45. MAKE IT A WORK OF ART****45.使之成为艺术品**

Forget about utility, functionality and practicality.抛开实用性、功能性和实用性不谈。

Approach the project as an artist would.以艺术家的方式对待项目。

Think beauty. 想想美。

Think craftsmanship. 想想手工艺。

Put yourself in the shoes of a sculptor, a maker or a photographer.设身处地为雕塑家、制作者或摄影师着想。

Try being a street-artist, a calligrapher or an animator.尝试成为街头艺术家、书法家或动画师。

See the task through the eyes of a fashion designer, a writer or a musician.用时装设计师、作家或音乐家的眼光来看待这项任务。

The goal is to cook up a whole new style, look or sound.我们的目标是创造出一种全新的风格、外观或声音。

Create something that has never existed before, something beautiful and stirring, worth writing home about.创造前所未有的东西，创造美丽动人的东西，创造值得大书特书的东西。

If you don’t have the skills yourself, then hire someone who can push the craft to the limit.如果你自己不具备这种技能，那就雇一个能将工艺发挥到极致的人。
