**30. MAKE SOMEONE’S DAY****30.让某人过上好日子**

Pick any one member of your target audience and make their dream come true.从目标受众中任选一人，让他们梦想成真。

It could be someone deserving who just never had the opportunity to shine.这可能是一个值得尊敬的人，只是从来没有机会大放异彩。

Or it could be a name you picked at random.也可能是你随意取的名字。

Your brand could either help that person directly or it could act as a facilitator and rally the community (either in the neighborhood or online) behind the effort.你的品牌既可以直接帮助这个人，也可以充当促进者，号召社区（社区或网络）支持这项工作。

The goal is to generate content that will be shared online.目标是生成可在网上分享的内容。

But the way you go about helping that person should clue your brand’s mission.但是，你帮助他人的方式应该反映出你的品牌使命。

And just as subtly make the world believe that your brand is one of the good guys.同样巧妙地让世界相信你的品牌是好人。
