**34. STAGE A SPECTACLE****34.上演奇观**

There are two reasons to go down this route.走这条路有两个原因。

The first is to get on the news and create some buzz around your brand.首先是上新闻，为自己的品牌造势。

The second is to generate content that will get passed around on social media.其次是制作能在社交媒体上传播的内容。

Your spectacle could be in the form of a public event, a roadshow, a PR stunt or a film, any of which deliver a payoff that is consistent with your brand promise.你的奇观可以是公共活动、路演、公关噱头或电影，任何一种形式都能带来与品牌承诺一致的回报。

Be ambitious with your spectacle.对你的奇观要有雄心壮志。

If possible, aim to set a world record.如果可能，目标是创造世界纪录。
