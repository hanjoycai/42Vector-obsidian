**16. COMPARE AND CONTRAST****16.对比**

Everything is relative. 一切都是相对的。

People can’t judge the value of something in isolation.人们不能孤立地判断某样东西的价值。

They have to compare it with other things, consciously or unconsciously.他们不得不自觉或不自觉地将其与其他事物进行比较。

So, as a marketer, you can create value by manipulating the benchmark and carefully selecting the things that are compared.因此，作为营销人员，您可以通过操纵基准和精心选择比较对象来创造价值。

However, comparison advertising shouldn’t just mean listing the weaknesses of competitors’ features or mocking their prices.然而，对比广告不应该只是罗列竞争对手的功能弱点或嘲弄其价格。

In fact, you don’t have to compare competing brands at all.事实上，您根本不需要对竞争品牌进行比较。

Compare places, circumstances and ideas.比较地点、环境和想法。

Compare attitudes, feelings and perspectives.比较态度、感受和观点。

Compare behavior, actions and results.比较行为、行动和结果。

Compare the past with the present, before with after, a ‘no’ with a ‘yes’.比较过去和现在，之前和之后，"不 "和 "是"。

Compare your brand with the entire category it’s in.将您的品牌与它所在的整个类别进行比较。

Be creative in how you present your comparisons too.在进行比较时也要有创意。

They can be side-by-side, one after the other, or mixed together.它们可以并排、相继或混合在一起。
