**40. INVITE PARTICIPATION****40.邀请参与**

When your viewers play an active role in your ad, they are more likely to help spread the message.当观众在广告中发挥积极作用时，他们更有可能帮助传播信息。

However, getting consumers to participate in advertising is easier said than done.然而，让消费者参与广告宣传说起来容易做起来难。

One way to motivate them is to ask for their opinion.激励他们的方法之一就是征求他们的意见。

Granted you’ll hear some views you didn’t want to hear, but that’s part of the deal.当然，你会听到一些你不想听到的观点，但这也是交易的一部分。

A second way is to ask people to be creative.第二种方法是要求人们发挥创造力。

A third is to get them to contribute towards building something together, perhaps an inspiring project that is so big that it calls for talent coming together from many parts of the globe.第三种方法是让他们为共同建设某项事业做出贡献，也许是一个鼓舞人心的大项目，需要全球各地的人才汇聚一堂。

And the final option is to create a platform that enables one group of perhaps privileged people to aid another not-so privileged group.最后一个选择是创建一个平台，让一群或许享有特权的人能够帮助另一群并不享有特权的人。

But no matter how you choose to go about it, you still have to think about how the participants are rewarded.但无论如何选择，都必须考虑如何奖励参与者。

Remember, the reward doesn’t always have to be material.记住，奖励不一定都是物质的。

It can be an emotional reward, say, the satisfaction of helping another human being.它可以是一种情感奖励，比如，帮助他人的满足感。
