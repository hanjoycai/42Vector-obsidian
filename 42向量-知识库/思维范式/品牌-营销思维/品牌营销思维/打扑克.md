**42. PLAY A PRANK****42.打扑克**

Get ready for some street-theatre.准备好欣赏街头戏剧吧

You’re going to subject a few unsuspecting people to a wild practical joke in order to highlight a key selling point of your product, then make an entertaining video from the footage that you hope will be shared online.为了突出产品的关键卖点，你要让几个毫无防备的人开一个疯狂的恶作剧，然后把这些镜头制作成一个娱乐视频，希望能在网上分享。

Prank films are entertaining because they pack action, drama, suspense and laughter in a single event.恶作剧电影之所以具有娱乐性，是因为它集动作、戏剧、悬念和欢笑于一体。

They can give a brand an aura of being rather anti-authoritarian and revolutionary.它们会给品牌带来一种反权威和革命性的光环。

Not only do they cost less than high-end television commercials, they are also more authentic and down-to-earth.与高端电视广告相比，它们不仅成本更低，而且更真实、更朴实。

But staging a successful prank is easier said than done.但是，要成功举办一场恶作剧，说起来容易做起来难。

You need great timing and emotional intuition.你需要很好的时机和情感直觉。

It’s only a good prank if there are laughs at the end, especially from the people at the receiving end.只有笑到最后才是好的恶作剧，尤其是接受恶作剧的人。
